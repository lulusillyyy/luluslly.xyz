I'm sure I'm not the first one to say this, but I genuinely think the Keyboardio Model 01 hits a lot of points -- to the point where I could easily tell you despite its flaws it stands as one of the most unique and one of my favorite keyboards out of my entire collection thus far. Let me elaborate further:

# A Keyboard Truly 'Heirloom-Grade'

When you see the promotional trailer for this keyboard, you see this pretty keyboard with the silly octopus tenting stands and a promise of an "heirloom-grade" ergonomic keyboard. In fact, when it came out, many keyboard nerds praised it not only for the wooden casing, but the genuinely pleasant typing experience. With I myself now owning one almost a decade after it's initial release, I can tell you from direct experience that those keyboard nerds are absolutely correct in their interpretation.

# Comparison to the Model 100

Honestly the Model 100 -- pictured below -- solves the primary issues that the Model 01 has. Specifically, it is hot-swappable and has a more modern microcontroller, as well as minor quality-of-life tweaks to marginally improve on the original design. If I were to tell a keyboard afficianado to buy either-or, I would definitely tell them to buy the Model 100 -- it's simply more versatile, and the ability to use your preferred switches instead of being locked into Matias' heavier clicky switches is absolutely a deal-breaker for a lot of people.
